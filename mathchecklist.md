# 📌 **Checklist for Pure Mathematics (with Dependencies and Books)**


## 🏆 **Core Subjects Part 1 (No Prerequisites)**  
These foundational courses introduce key mathematical structures.  

### 📌 **Linear Algebra**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Vector spaces, systems of linear equations  
- Bases, linear independence  
- Matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, eigenvectors  
- Inner product spaces, orthogonality, Gram-Schmidt process  
- Diagonalization, Jordan canonical form  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Linear Algebra* – Friedberg, Insel, Spence  
- *Linear Algebra Done Right* – Axler  

---

### 📌 **Algebra I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Group theory: subgroups, cyclic groups, permutation groups, dihedral and alternating groups  
- Ring theory: rings, ideals, quotient rings, UFDs, PIDs, maximal/prime ideals, localization  
- Field theory: fields, field extensions  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Algebra: Notes from the Underground* – Paolo Aluffi  
- *Abstract Algebra* – Dummit & Foote  

---

### 📌 **Analysis I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Real number system, sequences, and series  
- Metric spaces: definitions, open and closed sets, neighborhoods  
- Limits, Continuity, Differentiation, Riemann integration  
- Uniform convergence, power series  
- Introduction to differential forms and Stokes’ theorem  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Calculus* - Michael Spivak
- *Understanding Analysis* – Abbott  
- *Real Mathematical Analysis* – Pugh  
- *Principles of Mathematical Analysis (Baby Rudin)* – Rudin  
- *Multivariable Mathematics* – Shifrin (for analysis on **R**^n)

---

### 📌 **Point-Set Topology**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None (but Analysis I helps)  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Topological spaces, open and closed sets  
- Continuity, compactness, connectedness  
- Separation axioms, countability, metrization  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Topology* – Munkres  
- *Introduction to Topological Manifolds* – John M. Lee (requires Analysis I)  

---

## 🏛 **Core Subjects Part 2 (Linear Algebra Required for All)**  

### 📌 **Analysis II (Real Analysis & Measure Theory)**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Analysis I
📚 **Books:**  
- *Real and Complex Analysis (Big Rudin)* – Walter Rudin  
- *Real Analysis: Modern Techniques and Applications* – Folland  

---

### 📌 **Algebra II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebra I  
📚 **Books:**  
- *Algebra* – Serge Lang  
- *Algebra: Chapter 0* – Paolo Aluffi  

---

### 📌 **Algebraic Topology**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Point-Set Topology  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Fundamental group, covering spaces  
- Homology theory, exact sequences  
- Applications to geometry and fixed-point theorems  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Algebraic Topology* – Hatcher  
- *Introduction to Topological Manifolds* – John M. Lee  

---

### 📌 **Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Parametric curves and surfaces, tangent vectors  
- Curvature and torsion of curves, Frenet-Serret formulas  
- First and second fundamental forms, Gaussian curvature  
- Theorema Egregium, Gauss-Bonnet theorem  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Differential Geometry: A First Course* – Shifrin  
- *Elementary Differential Geometry* – Pressley  

### 📌 **Ordinary Differential Equations**
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📚 **Books:**
- *Differential Equations, Dynamical Systems and an Introduction to Chaos* - Hirsch, Smale, Devany
- *Ordinary Differential Equations* - Arnold

### 📌 **Fourier Analysis (Central to Physics & PDEs)**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Analysis I, Linear Algebra  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Fourier series and integrals  
- Convergence of Fourier series  
- Fourier transform & applications in physics  
- Distributions and generalized functions  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Fourier Analysis: An Introduction* – Stein & Shakarchi  

---

### 📌 **Functional Analysis (Foundation for Quantum Mechanics & PDEs)**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Analysis II, Linear Algebra  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Banach and Hilbert spaces  
- Operators, Spectral Theorem  
- Hahn-Banach Theorem, Open Mapping Theorem  
- Applications to quantum mechanics  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Introductory Functional Analysis with Applications* – Kreyszig  
- *A Course in Functional Analysis* – Conway  
- *Functional Analysis* – Rudin  

---

### 📌 **Partial Differential Equations (Essential for Physics & Engineering)**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Fourier Analysis, Functional Analysis  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Classification of PDEs (Elliptic, Parabolic, Hyperbolic)  
- Laplace, Heat, and Wave Equations  
- Green’s Functions, Characteristics Method  
- Sobolev Spaces and Weak Solutions  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Partial Differential Equations* – Evans  
- *Fourier Series and Boundary Value Problems* – Brown & Churchill  
- *Introduction to Partial Differential Equations* – Folland  

### 📌 **Algebraic Number Theory**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebra I  
📚 **Books:**  
- *Algebraic Number Theory and Fermat’s Last Theorem* – Stewart, Tall  
- *Problems in Algebraic Number Theory* – Esmonde, Murty

- *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds* – John M. Lee
