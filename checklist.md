# 🏆 **Core Subjects Part 1 (No Prerequisites)**  
These foundational courses introduce key mathematical structures.  

## 📌 **Linear Algebra**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Vector spaces, systems of linear equations  
✅ Bases, linear independence  
✅ Matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, eigenvectors  
✅ Inner product spaces, orthogonality, Gram-Schmidt process  
✅ Diagonalization, Jordan canonical form  

## 📌 **Algebra I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Group theory: subgroups, cyclic groups, permutation groups, dihedral and alternating groups,  
✅ Ring theory: rings, ideals, quotient rings, unique factorization domain, Euclidean domain, integral domain, maximal and prime ideals, rings of fractions.
✅ Field theory: fields, field extensions  

## 📌 **Analysis I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Real number system, sequences, and series  
✅ Metric spaces: definitions, open and closed sets, neighborhoods  
✅ Continuity, differentiability, Riemann integration  
✅ Uniform convergence, power series  

## 📌 **Point-Set Topology**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** None  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Topological spaces, open and closed sets  
✅ Continuity, compactness, connectedness  
✅ Separation axioms, countability, metrization  

---

# 🏛 **Core Subjects Part 2 (Linear Algebra Required for All)**  
Advanced topics that build upon Part 1.  

## 📌 **Analysis II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Multivariable calculus: partial derivatives, multiple integrals  
✅ Introduction to differential forms and manifolds  
✅ Generalized Stokes' theorem  
✅ Metric spaces: completeness, compactness  
✅ Function spaces, Arzelà–Ascoli theorem  
✅ Fourier series, Lebesgue integration  

## 📌 **Algebra II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebra I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Advanced group theory: Sylow theorems, group actions  
✅ Module theory over rings, structure theorem for modules over PID  
✅ Galois theory: solvability by radicals, fundamental theorem of Galois theory  

## 📌 **Algebraic Topology I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Point-Set Topology  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Fundamental group, covering spaces  
✅ Homology theory, exact sequences  
✅ Applications to geometry and fixed-point theorems  

## 📌 **Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Parametric curves and surfaces, tangent vectors  
✅ Curvature and torsion of curves, Frenet-Serret formulas  
✅ First and second fundamental forms, Gaussian curvature  
✅ Theorema Egregium, Gauss-Bonnet theorem  

---

# 🌟 **Sigma Subjects (All Core Subjects Required)**  
Advanced courses requiring mastery of core material.  

## 📌 **Manifolds**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Analysis II, Algebraic Topology I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Definitions and examples of manifolds  
✅ Smooth maps, diffeomorphisms  
✅ Tangent and cotangent bundles, vector fields  
✅ Differential forms, exterior calculus  
✅ Integration on manifolds, Stokes' theorem  

## 📌 **Algebraic Topology II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebraic Topology I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Cohomology theory, cup and cap products  
✅ Poincaré duality, homotopy theory  
✅ Spectral sequences, characteristic classes  

## 📌 **Differential Geometry**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Manifolds  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Riemannian metrics, connections, geodesics  
✅ Curvature tensors, sectional curvature  
✅ Einstein manifolds, Ricci flow  

## 📌 **Algebraic Geometry**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebra II, Commutative Algebra  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Affine and projective varieties  
✅ Morphisms, rational maps, divisors  
✅ Sheaf theory, schemes, cohomology

# 🔢 **Advanced Algebra & Number Theory Track**  

## 📌 **Commutative Algebra**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebra II  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Noetherian rings, primary decomposition  
✅ Integral extensions, valuation rings  
✅ Dimension theory, Hilbert's Nullstellensatz  

## 📌 **Number Theory**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Algebra II  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
✅ Diophantine equations, prime number theorem  
✅ Modular forms, elliptic curves  
✅ Algebraic number fields, class field theory
