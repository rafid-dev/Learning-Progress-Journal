# 📌 **Checklist Theoretical Physics (with Dependencies and Books)**

## 🏆 **Core Subjects Part 1 (Undergraduate Level - No Physical Prerequisites)**  

### 📌 **1. Classical Mechanics**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Newtonian Mechanics  
- Lagrangian & Hamiltonian Formulation  
- Small Oscillations, Normal Modes  
- Central Force Motion  
- Rigid Body Dynamics  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Classical Mechanics* – John R. Taylor  
- *Mechanics* – Landau & Lifshitz  

---

### 📌 **2. Electromagnetism I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Electrostatics & Magnetostatics  
- Maxwell’s Equations  
- Electromagnetic Waves  
- Special Relativity & Covariant Formulation  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Introduction to Electrodynamics* – David J. Griffiths  
- *Electricity and Magnetism* – Purcell & Morin  

---

### 📌 **3. Waves & Optics**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Mechanical & Electromagnetic Waves  
- Interference, Diffraction  
- Polarization, Dispersion  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Optics* – Eugene Hecht  
- *The Physics of Vibrations and Waves* – H. John Pain  

---

### 📌 **4. Quantum Mechanics I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Wave-Particle Duality  
- Schrödinger Equation  
- Operators, Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors  
- Angular Momentum, Spin  
- Approximation Methods  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics* – David J. Griffiths  
- *Principles of Quantum Mechanics* – R. Shankar  

---

### 📌 **5. Statistical Mechanics & Thermodynamics**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Linear Algebra, Analysis I  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Laws of Thermodynamics  
- Boltzmann Distribution  
- Quantum Statistics: Bose-Einstein & Fermi-Dirac  
- Phase Transitions & Critical Phenomena  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Statistical Mechanics* – R.K. Pathria & P.D. Beale  
- *Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics* – Greiner  

---

## 🚀 **Core Subjects Part 2 (Intermediate Level - Advanced Mathematics Required)**  

### 📌 **6. Classical Mechanics II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Classical Mechanics I, Ordinary Differential Equations
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Poisson Brackets, Canonical Transformations  
- Hamilton-Jacobi Theory  
- Action-Angle Variables  
- Chaos in Classical Systems  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Classical Mechanics* – Goldstein  
- *Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics* – V.I. Arnold (Requires strong mathematical background:- Algebra, Manifolds, Differential Geometry)  

---

### 📌 **7. Electromagnetism II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Electromagnetism I, Analysis II  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Relativistic Electrodynamics  
- Radiation & Wave Propagation  
- Green’s Functions & Boundary Value Problems  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Classical Electrodynamics* – J.D. Jackson  
- *Modern Electrodynamics* – Andrew Zangwill  

---

### 📌 **8. Quantum Mechanics II**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Quantum Mechanics I, Analysis II  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Scattering Theory  
- Path Integrals  
- Identical Particles & Quantum Statistics  
- Symmetries in Quantum Mechanics  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Principles of Quantum Mechanics* – R. Shankar  
- *Modern Quantum Mechanics* – J.J. Sakurai  

---

### 📌 **9. First Course in General Relativity**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Electromagnetism I, Classical Mechanics I, Analysis I, Linear Algebra
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Tensors & Curved Spacetime  
- Einstein’s Field Equations  
- Black Hole Physics & Gravitational Waves  
- Cosmological Models  

📚 **Books:**  
*A First Course in General Relativity* – Bernard Schutz 

---

## 🌟 **Advanced Track (Graduate Level)**  

### 📌 **10. General Relativity**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Manifolds, Differential Geometry, Electromagnetism II
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Tensors & Curved Spacetime  
- Einstein’s Field Equations  
- Black Hole Physics & Gravitational Waves  
- Cosmological Models  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Spacetime and Geometry* – Sean Carroll  
- *General Relativity* – Robert Wald

### 📌 **11. Quantum Field Theory I**  
🛠 **Prerequisites:** Quantum Mechanics II, Special Relativity  
📖 **Content Overview:**  
- Classical Field Theory, Noether’s Theorem  
- Second Quantization, Feynman Diagrams  
- Renormalization, Path Integrals  

📚 **Books:**  
- *Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell* – A. Zee  
- *Peskin & Schroeder – An Introduction to QFT*
